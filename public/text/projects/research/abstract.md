My research was a continuation of Branson Jones' work in the Summer of 2024 and Spring of 2025. His goal was to model the formation of Cirrus Cloud Ice Crystals (which was defined by a set of partial differential equations). He was training a Physics Encoded Neural Network ("PINN"), but never got his model to converge. My work was on specifically using a recurrent architecture in hopes that the recursive nature of the formula would help.
